## 3. Formal Counsel Engagement Scope & Parameters

### 3.1 Requested Jurisdictions
Outside counsel is requested to conduct clearance and risk analysis across:
1. **United States (Primary):** USPTO Federal Trademark Register (Principal & Supplemental), state trademark registries, and federal common-law databases.
2. **India (Primary):** Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks (IP India) in Class 09 and Class 42.
3. **European Union & United Kingdom (Secondary):** EUIPO eSearch Plus and UKIPO trademark databases.

### 3.2 Target Nice Classifications
- **Class 09 (Core Software):** Downloadable computer software; downloadable machine learning libraries; algorithms recorded on machine-readable media.
- **Class 42 (Technology Services):** Software as a Service (SaaS); cloud computing; consultancy in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

### 3.3 Requested Delivery Format
Counsel is requested to deliver a formal, written legal opinion letter on law firm letterhead containing:
1. Executive Summary & Freedom-to-Operate (FTO) Conclusion.
2. Detailed analysis of each of the 10 questions in [`COUNSEL_QUESTIONNAIRE_FINAL.md`](file:///c:/projects/AdaptiveML/docs/verification/stage_16_3_1/COUNSEL_QUESTIONNAIRE_FINAL.md).
3. Search report exhibit detailing searched marks, citations, and conflicting serial numbers.
4. Specific actionable recommendation on candidate mark *ResiliML*.
5. Written conditions precedent for controlled Alpha release (Phase 17) and public PyPI release (Phase 18).

### 3.4 Requested Delivery Timeline
- **Target Engagement Date:** To be agreed upon formal retention by the Project Owner.
- **Target Opinion Delivery:** Estimated 10 to 15 business days following formal engagement and delivery of search reports.
