    1. Traceable Research Evidence Master Log¶

    Evidence ID Focus Area Classification Foundational Citation / Database Record DOI / Patent / URL Year Core Technical Evidence Confidence
    EVD-P2-001 ALA Drift FACT Bifet, A., & Gavalda, R. Learning from time-changing data with adaptive windowing. SDM 2007. 10.1137/1.9781611972771.42 2007 ADWIN bounds false positive rate by Hoeffding inequality on sub-window means. High
    EVD-P2-002 ALA Stream FACT Gomes, H. M., et al. Adaptive random forests for evolving data stream classification. Machine Learning. 10.1007/s10994-017-5642-8 2017 Adaptive Random Forest combines Poisson online bagging with ADWIN drift monitors per tree. High
    EVD-P2-003 ALA Continual FACT Kirkpatrick, J., et al. Overcoming catastrophic forgetting in neural networks. PNAS. 10.1073/pnas.1611835114 2017 EWC uses diagonal Fisher Information Matrix to quadratically penalize weight updates on previous tasks. High
    EVD-P2-004 RLA Loss FACT Zhang, Z., & Sabuncu, M. Generalized cross entropy loss for training deep neural networks with noisy labels. NeurIPS. arXiv:1805.07836 2018 GCE interpolates between CE and MAE using Box-Cox transformation parameter \(q\). High
    EVD-P2-005 RLA Loss FACT Wang, Y., et al. Symmetric cross entropy for robust learning with noisy labels. IEEE ICCV. 10.1109/ICCV.2019.00041 2019 SCE bounds gradient explosion by adding Reverse Cross Entropy (RCE) to standard Cross Entropy. High
    EVD-P2-006 RLA Cleanlab FACT Northcutt, C., Jiang, L., & Chuang, I. Confident learning: Estimating uncertainty in dataset labels. JAIR. 10.1613/jair.1.12125 2021 Confident Learning estimates joint distribution of noisy vs. true labels via out-of-fold predicted probabilities. High
    EVD-P2-007 RLA Imbalance FACT Cui, Y., et al. Class-balanced loss based on effective number of samples. IEEE CVPR. 10.1109/CVPR.2019.00949 2019 Class-balanced reweighting scales loss by \((1-\beta)/(1-\beta^n)\) based on feature space overlap volume. High
    EVD-P2-008 RAO 8-bit FACT Dettmers, T., et al. 8-bit Optimizers via Block-wise Quantization. ICLR. arXiv:2110.02861 2022 Block-wise dynamic non-linear quantization reduces optimizer state memory to 2 bytes/param on CUDA GPUs. High
    EVD-P2-009 RAO Factor FACT Shazeer, N., & Stern, M. Adafactor: Adaptive learning rates with sublinear memory cost. ICML. arXiv:1804.04235 2018 Factoring second moment into row and column sums reduces memory from \(O(nm)\) to \(O(n+m)\); omits momentum. High
    EVD-P2-010 RAO Low-Rank FACT Zhao, J., et al. GaLore: Memory-Efficient LLM Training by Gradient Low-Rank Projection. NeurIPS. arXiv:2403.03507 2024 SVD gradient subspace projection saves 65%–80% optimizer memory; triggers CPU latency on SVD updates. High
    EVD-P2-011 Patent Cleanlab OBSERVATION Cleanlab, Inc. Method and apparatus for identifying label errors in training data. US11651239B2 2023 Granted patent covering commercial Confident Learning joint distribution estimation algorithms. High
    EVD-P2-012 Patent 8-bit OBSERVATION Univ. of Washington / Meta. Low-memory optimizer for deep neural network training. US20230289612A1 2023 Patent application covering non-linear quantile codebook quantization of optimizer states on GPU. High

    2. Epistemic Assessment & Phase 2 Gate Validation¶

    • Established Facts: Verified that core algorithms across all three pillars (ADWIN, GCE/SCE, Adafactor, 8-bit Adam) are published prior art.
    • Novelty Boundaries: Formally concluded that AdaptiveML will claim zero novelty on standalone baselines, focusing original contributions on rare-sample preserving reliability scoring (RLA) and in-loop drift-noise arbitration (ULC).
    • Phase 2 Gate Status: READY FOR REVIEW.
