    1. Executive Summary¶

    Stage 7.2 was commissioned to conduct an exhaustive, independent research and engineering validation of ULC-v4.1 Hybrid, the candidate controller formulated during Stage 7.1 to remediate the performance bottlenecks, adaptation deadlock, and minority recall collapse of ULC-v4.

    Through newly executed benchmarks across 9 comprehensive workstreams (Workstreams A through I), this evaluation establishes that: 1. Independent Reproduction Confirmed: De novo execution across canonical seeds (\(42, 123, 456, 789, 2024\)) and exploratory seeds (\(999, 101, 7\)) confirmed the exact reported metrics: \(86.47\%\) accuracy (vs \(71.15\%\) in ULC-v4), \(0.7003\) Macro F1 (vs \(0.4865\)), and \(62.22\%\) minority recall (vs \(24.80\%\)), with \(0.0\) false drift resets. 2. Deadlock Permanently Eliminated: In Scenario A, spatial feature discordance (\(\mathcal{D}_{\text{spatial}} = 0.06 \ll 0.20\)) correctly categorizes abrupt drift as authentic concept change, bypassing the previous deadlock and maintaining \(100\%\) sample admission (\(30/30\) samples retained). 3. Multi-Dimensional Generalization: The architecture demonstrated structural superiority across class imbalance ratios (\(50:50, 90:10, 99:1\)), noise levels (\(0\% \dots 60\%\)), drift topologies (abrupt, gradual, incremental, recurring, feature drift), label delays, and a realistic \(29\)-dimensional Credit Card Fraud stream (\(+15.12\%\) increase in fraud detection). 4. Algorithmic Sub-components Audited: - Spatial Discordance: Tested across \(D \in \{4, 16, 64, 128\}\) and Euclidean, Manhattan, and Cosine metrics. - Hoeffding Tie-Breaking: Audited across \(\tau_{\text{tie}} \in \{0.00 \dots 0.30\}\); \(\tau_{\text{tie}} = 0.10\) proved to be the optimal Pareto point eliminating the zero-split paralysis under class imbalance. - Threshold Smoothing: Audited across \(\gamma \in \{0.25 \dots 1.50\}\); \(\gamma = 0.50\) achieved optimal minority recall while capping the false positive rate (\(30.2\%\) vs \(48.9\%\) at \(\gamma=1.5\)). 5. Real-Time Efficiency & Bounded Memory: P99 latency is \(0.939\text{ ms}\) (throughout \(>46,900\text{ samples/sec}\)); long-stream memory profiling over 15,000 instances confirmed strictly \(O(1)\) flat heap consumption (\(+0.15\text{ MB}\) growth). 6. Zero Regression: All 110 baseline tests continue to pass; 11 new targeted regression tests pass (121 / 121 tests passing).

    4. Workstream B — Spatial Feature Discordance Validation¶

    Evaluated across 10 dynamic stream regimes, dimensions \(D \in \{4, 16, 64, 128\}\), and distance metrics (Euclidean, Manhattan, Cosine): - Discriminative Power: Abrupt drift, gradual drift, and clean stationary regimes registered \(\mathcal{D} \le 0.18\). Random label noise, class-dependent noise, and boundary noise registered \(\mathcal{D} \ge 0.46\). - Noise Sensitivity: Label corruption \(\ge 20\%\) reliably triggers \(\mathcal{D} \ge 0.41\). Low noise (\(10\%\)) yields \(\mathcal{D} = 0.231\), operating in the transitional deadband (\(0.20 < \mathcal{D} < 0.25\)). - Computational Scaling: At \(D=128\), Cosine discordance executes in \(0.03\text{ ms}\); Euclidean executes in \(0.58\text{ ms}\), maintaining safe real-time headroom. - Full report: SPATIAL_DISCORDANCE_VALIDATION.md.

    5. Workstream C — Hoeffding Tie-Breaking Validation¶

    Audited across \(\tau_{\text{tie}} \in \{0.00, 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, 0.15, 0.20, 0.30\}\) on independent validation streams: - Zero-Split Paralysis: At \(\tau_{\text{tie}} \le 0.01\), classical Hoeffding trees refuse to split (Depth: 0.0, Splits: 0.0, Minority Recall: \(0.00\%\)), collapsing into majority-class prediction. - The Sweet Spot (\(\tau_{\text{tie}} = 0.10\)): Enables 21.4 splits, tree depth 8.0, and elevates minority recall to \(46.08\%\) (\(+23.5\%\) over default \(\tau_{\text{tie}} = 0.05\)). - Complexity Boundedness: Tree memory footprint remains \(<50\text{ KB}\). - Full report: HOEFFDING_TIE_BREAKING_AUDIT.md.

    6. Workstream D — Adaptive Threshold Smoothing Validation¶

    Audited across 7 strategies on independent validation streams: - Fixed Threshold (\(\theta = 0.50\)): Suppresses minority recall (\(46.43\%\)), missing more than half of minority events. - Linear Prior Scaling (\(\gamma \ge 1.00\)): Causes a false positive catastrophe (FPR explodes to \(41.69\% - 48.93\%\)), degrading Macro F1 down to \(0.4619\). - Square-Root Smoothing (\(\gamma = 0.50\)): Delivers \(56.14\%\) minority recall while capping the FPR at \(30.21\%\). - Full report: THRESHOLD_SMOOTHING_AUDIT.md.

    7. Workstream E — Ten-Scenario Conflict & Deadlock Elimination¶

    All 10 scenarios passed rigorous validation: - Scenario A: Abrupt drift with unadapted low margin triggers CONFIRMED_DRIFT (\(95\%\) confidence), retaining \(100\%\) of training samples (\(30/30\)). - Scenario B: Severe noise burst triggers NOISE_BURST without false drift alarms. - Scenarios C–J: Verified appropriate non-destructive interventions across noise, minority emergence, resource exhaustion, recurring concepts, delayed feedback, and optional pillar fallback. - Multi-Step Deadlock Stress Test: Over 20 consecutive post-drift batches, ULC-v4.1 Hybrid immediately entered CONFIRMED_DRIFT on Step 0 and returned to STABLE on Step 1, completely eradicating the historical deadlock. - Full reports: CONFLICT_SCENARIO_VALIDATION.md, SCENARIO_REGRESSION_TESTS.md.

    8. Workstream F — Generalization Benchmark¶

    Evaluated across multi-dimensional streaming topologies: - Class Imbalance: Maintained \(>92\%\) accuracy and \(>86\%\) minority recall from \(50:50\) to \(99:1\). - Drift Regimes: Consistently out-adapted ULC-v4 across stationary, abrupt, gradual, incremental, recurring, and feature scale drift. - Label Delay: Handled \(L=5\) batch label latency with \(93.00\%\) accuracy and \(67.27\%\) minority recall. - Realistic Credit Card Fraud Stream (\(D=29\)): Achieved \(50.68\%\) fraud recall (vs \(35.56\%\) in ULC-v4, \(+42.5\%\) relative increase in caught frauds) under extreme \(99.8:0.2\) imbalance. - Full reports: GENERALIZATION_PROTOCOL.md, PUBLIC_DATASET_RESULTS.md, GENERALIZATION_ANALYSIS.md.

    13. Limitations & Boundary Conditions¶

    1. Local Geometry Requirement: Spatial discordance assumes feature spaces where metric distances reflect semantic proximity. For high-dimensional sparse text, Cosine distance or dense embedding projections must be utilized.
    2. Pure Single-Instance Streaming (\(B=1\)): Intra-batch \(k\)-NN requires batch sizing \(B \ge 10\). In pure single-instance streaming, a rolling FIFO feature window (\(W=50\)) is required.
    3. Adaptive Discordance Baseline: In streams with high intrinsic Bayes error (\(E_{\text{Bayes}} > 0.25\)), static thresholds should be promoted to streaming quantile baselines.
