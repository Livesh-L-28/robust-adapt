    AdaptiveML — Stage 12: Workstream F Research Report¶

    Cross-Pillar Research: Compound Dynamics, Coupling Interactions & Systemic Trade-Offs¶

    Workspace: C:\projects\AdaptiveML
    Distribution: aml-core (adaptive_ml) v0.1.0.dev0
    Baseline Git Commit: 67e9ef1b347ca223828a1f225e85e35a086a1f30 (Working tree clean/preserved)
    Research Artifact: experiments/stage_12/cross_pillar/run_cross_pillar_experiment.py
    Primary Dataset: Electricity (Elec2) Streaming Dataset (\(N=45,312\) instances, \(B=50\))
    Status: VALIDATED & COMPLETED

    1. Executive Summary & Research Motivation¶

    In Stages 2–11, each pillar of AdaptiveML (ALA, RLA, RAO, Minority Intelligence) was developed and evaluated largely in isolation or in pairwise combinations. Stage 12 Workstream F systematically evaluates how the refined 3.0/2.0 pillars interact when coupled into a unified streaming system under ULC-v5 orchestration: - Positive Synergy: Does combining ALA's recurring memory with RLA's noise filtering and Minority's budget thresholding produce mutual reinforcement? - Destructive Interference / Negative Coupling: Do conservative filtering thresholds in RLA conflict with adaptation signals in ALA, or does minority cost weighting destabilize low-memory optimizer quantization?

    2. Experimental Design & Configuration Matrix¶

    Evaluated configurations on the Electricity streaming benchmark across 10 paired seeds (\(S \in \{42, 101, 202, 303, 404, 505, 606, 707, 808, 909\}\)): 1. ala_3_0_alone: Recurring-concept memory (\(\tau=0.82, K=5\)) with standalone Hoeffding classifier. 2. rla_3_0_alone: Class-conditional noise filtering and reliability estimation without drift adaptation. 3. minority_2_0_alone: Bayesian prior tracking and PID threshold management on static learner. 4. ala_plus_rla: Dual coupling of ALA 3.0 recurring memory and RLA 3.0 reliability filtering. 5. rla_plus_minority: Dual coupling of RLA 3.0 robust filtering and Minority 2.0 dynamic budgeting. 6. full_next_gen_system: Unified 4-pillar system orchestrated by ULC-v5 (Multi-tier utility arbitration + ALA 3.0 memory + RLA 3.0 + Minority 2.0).

    4. Confirmatory Statistical Verification & Discovery of Negative Interactions¶

    From experiments/stage_12/statistics/run_stage_12_statistical_analysis.py: 1. Full System vs RLA Alone: - Prequential accuracy difference: \(+0.0889\) (\(+8.89\) percentage points). - Paired Cohen's \(d_z = +30.48\), \(t = 96.38\), \(p = 7.06 \times 10^{-15}\) (\(p < 0.001\)). 2. Full System vs ALA Alone (Negative Coupling Discovery): - Prequential accuracy difference: \(-0.0140\) (\(-1.40\) percentage points, from \(74.75\% \to 73.35\%\)). - \(95\%\) Confidence Interval: \([-0.018, -0.010]\). - Paired Cohen's \(d_z = -2.32\), \(t = -7.34\), two-tailed \(p = 4.42 \times 10^{-5}\) (\(p < 0.001\)).

    Root-Cause Analysis of the \(-1.40\%\) Deficit¶

    Why does coupling all four pillars yield slightly lower accuracy than ALA 3.0 alone on the Electricity stream? 1. Pillar Over-Filtering: On the Electricity stream, concept drift is the dominant non-stationary phenomenon; adversarial label noise is absent. 2. Conservative Abstention: When RLA 3.0 operates on clean drifting data, its class-conditional anomaly detector occasionally misinterprets newly drifting features as label noise, filtering or downweighting valid concept-shift samples before they reach the ALA fingerprinter. 3. Double Damping: Concurrently, ULC-v5's parameter damping interacts with Minority 2.0's threshold manager, causing slight under-adaptation. 4. Variance Reduction vs Peak Accuracy: While Full System accuracy is \(1.40\%\) lower than ALA alone, its variance across seeds is cut by more than half (\(\pm 0.28\%\) vs \(\pm 0.60\%\)), and hard resets are completely eliminated (\(0.0\) vs \(53.6\)).

    5. Architectural Recommendations¶

    • Dynamic Pillar Gating: Production controllers must not activate all four pillars at maximum sensitivity simultaneously. Instead, ULC must dynamically gate RLA and Minority weighting based on observed noise and class-imbalance telemetry. When stream imbalance is mild (\(\text{IR} < 2:1\)) and noise telemetry indicates clean data, RLA filtering should enter pass-through mode, freeing ALA to adapt unimpeded.

    6. Research Conclusion & Next Steps¶

    Cross-pillar evaluation provides empirical evidence of both massive synergistic gain (\(+8.89\%\) over raw robust models) and a critical negative interaction boundary (\(-1.40\%\) when running unfiltered RLA on clean concept-drifting streams). This discovery directly informs Stage 13 controller design.
