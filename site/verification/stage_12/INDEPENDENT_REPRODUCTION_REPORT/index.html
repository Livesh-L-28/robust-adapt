    1. Executive Summary & Verification Environment¶

    Stage 12 was conducted under strict scientific reproducibility standards. All datasets, algorithms, benchmark harnesses, seeds, and statistical tests are fully committed to the workspace and independently executable without network access.

    Host Execution Environment¶

    • Operating System: Microsoft Windows 11 Enterprise (Build 26100, AMD64)
    • Primary Runtime: Python 3.14.3 (64-bit)
    • Key Installed Dependencies:
    • torch: 2.12.1+cpu
    • scikit-learn: 1.8.0
    • pandas: 3.0.1
    • scipy: 1.17.0
    • numpy: 2.2.3

    3. Step-by-Step Reproduction Commands¶

    To replicate the entire Stage 12 research and engineering validation suite, execute the following commands in the workspace root:

    Step 1: Workstream A — ALA 3.0 Benchmark (Electricity Stream)¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/ala_3_0/run_ala_3_0_electricity_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 35\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/ala_3_0_electricity_results.json and .csv

    Step 2: Workstream B — RLA 3.0 Benchmark (Spambase Evasion)¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/rla_3_0/run_rla_3_0_spambase_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 20\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/rla_3_0_spambase_results.json and .csv

    Step 3: Workstream C — Minority Intelligence 2.0 Benchmark (Credit Card Fraud)¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/minority_2_0/run_minority_2_0_creditcard_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 45\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/minority_2_0_creditcard_results.json and .csv

    Step 4: Workstream D — RAO 2.0 Vectorized Benchmark (CoverTypeMLP)¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/rao_2_0/run_rao_2_0_covertype_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 15\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/rao_2_0_covertype_results.json and .csv

    Step 5: Workstream E — ULC-v5 Calibrated Benchmark (Electricity Stream)¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/ulc_v5/run_ulc_v5_electricity_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 40\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/ulc_v5_electricity_results.json and .csv

    Step 6: Workstream F — Cross-Pillar Research Benchmark¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/cross_pillar/run_cross_pillar_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 90\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/cross_pillar_results.json and .csv

    Step 7: Workstream G — Controlled Ablation Studies¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/ablations/run_ablations_experiment.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 75\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/ablation_results.json and .csv

    Step 8: Workstream H — Confirmatory Statistical Hypothesis Testing¶

    py -3.14 experiments/stage_12/statistics/run_stage_12_statistical_analysis.py
    - Runtime: \(\sim 2\text{ seconds}\) - Generates: experiments/stage_12/results/stage_12_statistical_summary.json and .csv
