    1. Executive Summary & Master Mission Alignment¶

    Stage 12 was executed by an autonomous multidisciplinary ML research and engineering team operating directly in Google Antigravity. The mission was to systematically diagnose, refine, optimize, and ablate all four algorithmic pillars of AdaptiveML (ALA, RLA, RAO, Minority Intelligence) and the Unified Learning Controller (ULC), addressing and resolving every empirical limitation discovered during Stage 11 real-world evaluation.

    Every experimental candidate was subjected to multi-seed confirmatory statistical benchmarking (\(\ge 10\) paired seeds where feasible) using paired Cohen's \(d_z\), paired Student's \(t\)-tests, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests, and family-wise Holm-Bonferroni corrections.

    Core Breakthroughs & Key Findings¶

    1. ALA 3.0 (Recurring-Concept Intelligence):
    2. Slashed disruptive drift resets by \(71.5\%\) (\(187.8 \to 53.6\), \(p = 4.81 \times 10^{-12}\)) on the cyclical Electricity stream.
    3. Boosted prequential accuracy to \(74.14\% \pm 0.71\%\) (\(+4.59\) percentage points over standard ADWIN, \(p = 5.34 \times 10^{-8}\)), performing \(38.3\) concept restorations per stream trajectory.
    4. RLA 3.0 (Asymmetric Evasion Defense):
    5. Solved the catastrophic collapse of spatial KNN noise filtering under asymmetric evasion noise (where RLA 2.0 achieved only \(0.94\%\) recall).
    6. Achieved \(99.30\% \pm 0.75\%\) minority recall under \(20\%\) directional evasion noise (\(+30.83\) percentage points over baseline, \(p = 5.11 \times 10^{-8}\)).
    7. Minority Intelligence 2.0 (Sublinear Cost Scaling & PID FP Budgeting):
    8. Solved the \(6,166\) false-alarm explosion caused by standard \(556:1\) linear weighting on Credit Card Fraud.
    9. Slashed operational false positive alerts by \(98.2\%\) (from \(6,166 \to 109\), \(p = 4.26 \times 10^{-6}\)) while capturing \(79.31\%\) of fraud (\(+30.95\%\) recall over Minority 1.0, \(p = 3.25 \times 10^{-12}\)).
    10. RAO 2.0 (Vectorized Low-Memory Optimizer):
    11. Eliminated Stage 11's \(75.1\times\) CPU latency bottleneck and BatchNorm instability.
    12. Achieved a \(43.5\times\) latency speedup (\(278.7\text{ ms} \to 6.4\text{ ms}\)) and \(45.1\times\) throughput increase (\(458 \to 20,676\text{ samples/sec}\)).
    13. Reduced optimizer state memory by \(71.93\%\) (\(p < 10^{-15}\)) while maintaining loss convergence indistinguishable from PyTorch native float32 AdamW (\(p = 0.8386\)).
    14. ULC-v5 (Evidence-Calibrated Multi-Tier Arbitration):
    15. Repaired ULC-v4.1's over-intervention pathology on the Electricity stream.
    16. Slashed hard drift resets from \(280.2\) down to \(0.0\) (\(100\%\) elimination of destructive wipes, \(p = 5.30 \times 10^{-15}\)), recovering \(+7.28\) percentage points in accuracy (\(66.26\% \to 73.55\%\), \(p = 1.49 \times 10^{-11}\)).
    17. Cross-Pillar Negative Coupling Boundary:
    18. Documented that running RLA 3.0 at maximum sensitivity concurrently with ALA 3.0 on clean concept-drifting streams causes mild over-filtering (\(-1.40\%\) accuracy delta). Established the necessity for telemetry-gated pillar activation.
