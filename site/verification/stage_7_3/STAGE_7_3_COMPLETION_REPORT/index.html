    1. Executive Summary¶

    Stage 7.3 has completed the controlled, reversible, and mathematically verified production promotion of ULC-v4.1 Hybrid into AdaptiveML's production codebase under adaptive_ml/ulc/.

    The absorbing deadlock defect in ULC-v4 (Scenario A where abrupt concept drift was misclassified as label noise whenever unadapted model margin fell below \(0.65\)) is provably eliminated. The newly integrated production controller UnifiedLearningControllerV4_1 reproduces the validated experimental candidate with exact statistical parity (\(86.47\% \pm 0.70\%\) accuracy, \(0.7003 \pm 0.0161\) macro F1, \(62.22\% \pm 5.78\%\) minority recall, and \(0\) false drift resets across the 5 canonical seeds), while all 147 unit, contract, integration, and regression tests pass with zero failures.
