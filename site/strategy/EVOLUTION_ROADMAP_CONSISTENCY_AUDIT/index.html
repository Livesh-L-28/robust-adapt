    1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate¶

    This formal consistency and governance audit was conducted in C:\projects\AdaptiveML to evaluate the newly generated Evolution Roadmap v2.0 suite.

    The audit focused on reconciling: 1. Initiative Count Discrepancies: Resolving the apparent conflict between the reported "47 proposed evolution items", the mapping ranges summing to 58, and the actual inventory of unique initiatives defined in the registers. 2. Authoritative Phase Preservation: Ensuring that the original 22-phase roadmap (docs/foundation/ROADMAP.md) is strictly preserved without reopening completed phases or altering historical acceptance criteria. 3. Phase Status & Governance Terminology: Decomposing phase statuses into six explicit governance dimensions (Implementation, Technical Verification, Deliverable Submission, Owner Acceptance, Legal Clearance, Release Authorization). 4. Academic Research & Industry Grounding: Verifying that academic citations are authentic, hypotheses are distinguished from established facts, and public dataset benchmarks are recognized as proxies rather than live operational deployments. 5. Brand Governance (DEF-004): Re-affirming that DEF-004 remains open, that suggested names (e.g., OpenAML) are unverified, and that package identity remains invariant (aml-core==0.1.0.dev0, adaptive_ml).

The "47" Figure: An early drafting tally counted only the prioritized core engineering, algorithmic improvements, and flagship applications, omitting the 7 standalone research directions (EVO-RES-001 through EVO-RES-007), 2 sector references (EVO-IND-005, EVO-IND-006), 4 deferred pillar items (EVO-ALA-006, EVO-RLA-006, EVO-RAO-006, EVO-ULC-006), and 4 deferred DX/Ecosystem items (EVO-DX-007, EVO-DX-008, EVO-ECO-006, EVO-ECO-007). 2. The "58" Figure: A subsequent partial synthesis added the 7 research items and some pillar items, but omitted several deferred opportunities and sector references (\(47 + 7 + 4 = 58\)). 3. The Definitive Canonical Count: A complete, automated parsing of all 9 evolution documents reveals exactly 64 unique initiatives.

    6. Phase Status & Governance Terminology Reconciliation¶

    Governance Evaluation Taxonomy:
1. Domain Work Completed: Research/design/code/packaging deliverable for the specific domain is finished.
2. Technical Verification Passed: Unit/integration tests pass (>= 90%), static analysis (Mypy strict), Bandit (0 findings).
3. Deliverables Submitted: Formal markdown reports and artifacts committed in docs/ or packaged in dist/.
4. Owner Acceptance: Formal sign-off by the Project Owner (ACCEPTED for Phase 0 baseline; PENDING for Phases 1–16).
5. Trademark Clearance: Decomposed governance gate (BLOCKED by DEF-004; no trademark clearance granted).
6. Release Authorization: Explicit approval to tag, publish, or release packages (HOLD / UNAUTHORIZED).

    7.2 Feasibility & Evidence Gaps Identified¶

    1. Computational Feasibility of CPU-Native 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4):
    2. Evidence Gap: On GPUs, NF4 utilizes ultra-fast warp-level shared memory lookup tables. On standard CPUs in pure NumPy, packing two 4-bit nibbles into a single uint8 byte requires bit-masking (val & 0x0F) and bit-shifting (val >> 4). In interpreted Python/NumPy, loop and array dispatch overhead could negate memory bandwidth savings.
    3. Mandatory Prerequisite: A C/NumPy micro-benchmark must measure step latency before asserting CPU speed gains over 8-bit quantization.
    4. Estimator Variance of Spatial Error Ratio \(\Lambda(t)\):
    5. Evidence Gap: In high dimensions (\(d > 50\)), local density and error variance estimation suffer from the curse of dimensionality, where pairwise Euclidean distances become equidistant.
    6. Mandatory Prerequisite: Verification must evaluate random projections or streaming Locality-Sensitive Hashing (LSH) to bound variance in high-dimensional manifolds.
    7. Early-Stream Variance of Online Peer Loss:
    8. Evidence Gap: During the first \(1,000\) steps of a cold stream, peer buffer samples may be auto-correlated, introducing non-zero gradient covariance.
    9. Mandatory Prerequisite: Warm-up schedules and peer buffer decorrelation lag \(\tau\) must be analyzed mathematically before claiming noise-rate agnosticism.

    8.3 Critical Benchmark Methodology Corrections¶

    1. Financial Fraud Temporal Delay Simulation: The ULB dataset contains relative seconds, but lacks transaction dispute resolution timestamps (real chargebacks occur 14–60 days after transaction). Any streaming evaluation that assumes immediate label availability suffers from catastrophic temporal lookahead leakage. The benchmark harness must explicitly simulate synthetic label arrival delay windows (\(\Delta t_{\text{feedback}}\)) to evaluate ALA and RLA validly.
    2. C-MAPSS Simulation Boundary: NASA C-MAPSS is a thermodynamically simulated dataset produced in MATLAB Simulink. It does not contain physical packet loss, sensor brownouts, or analog wire disconnection spikes. Realistic robust learning evaluations must inject synthetic telemetry dropout and sensor clipping.
