# ROBUST-ADAPT — Phase 18.1.5: Beta Privacy & Feedback Policy

**Project:** ROBUST-ADAPT (`robust-adapt`)<br>
**Target Release Candidate:** `1.0.0rc1`<br>
**Phase:** 18.1 — Gate 18.1.5: Privacy and Data Governance Policy<br>
**Status:** RATIFIED & ACTIVE

---

## 1. Core Privacy Commitments

ROBUST-ADAPT operates under a strict privacy-by-design architecture:

1. **Zero Mandatory Telemetry:** The library contains zero analytics beacons, error-reporting endpoints, phone-home mechanisms, or automated usage tracking. All execution occurs strictly on your local hardware.
2. **Zero Private Data Collection:** Participants must **never** submit confidential proprietary datasets, real-world customer records, or internal weights. All testing can and should be conducted using synthetic or public benchmark data streams.
3. **Voluntary Participation:** Engagement in the Beta evaluation cohort is entirely voluntary. Participants may withdraw at any time without justification.
4. **Pseudonymity & PII Protection:** Participant feedback will be recorded under pseudonyms (e.g., `Beta-Tester-01`, `Beta-Tester-02`). No personal contact details, IP addresses, or corporate affiliations will be published in public release records.

---

## 2. Permitted Use of Participant Feedback

Consented feedback provided by Beta participants will be utilized solely for:
- Identifying and remediating functional software bugs.
- Improving parameter naming and constructor ergonomics.
- Clarifying documentation, tutorials, and docstring examples.
- Publishing aggregated, anonymized usability metrics in release authorization dossiers.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Participant Rights

- **Storage:** Feedback notes and bug reports are stored in secure, access-controlled repositories.
- **Redaction & Deletion:** Any participant may request the immediate redaction or deletion of their submitted feedback prior to publication by contacting project maintainers.
- **Commercial Independence:** Feedback is collected exclusively for open-source software improvement under the Apache-2.0 license.
