## 1. Executive Summary & Non-Fabrication Mandate

Gate BETA.1 establishes the recruitment structure for the ROBUST-ADAPT private Beta program, requiring an external cohort of 5 to 10 independent Python machine-learning developers across six distinct professional personas.

### Strict Governance Rules:
- **No Agent Simulation:** AI agents, automated test harnesses, and project maintainers are explicitly excluded from counting as independent external participants.
- **No Fabricated Personas or Names:** Fictitious developer identities, simulated email addresses, or fabricated consent signatures are strictly forbidden.
- **Stage 1 Dependency:** Because Stage 1 legal clearance (DEF-004) and owner authorization are currently blocked, external dispatch of invitation packages is paused.
