## 3. Specific Jurisdiction Analysis Guidelines

### 3.1 United States (USPTO)
1. **Likelihood of Confusion Analysis (*In re E. I. du Pont de Nemours & Co.* factors):**
   - Similarity of marks in appearance, sound, connotation, and commercial impression.
   - Similarity of goods and services (downloadable ML software vs LLM alignment services).
   - Sophistication of buyers (enterprise ML engineers vs general public).
2. **Distinctiveness Assessment:**
   - Confirm mark is suggestive or arbitrary, not merely descriptive under § 2(e)(1).
3. **Common Law / State Register Evaluation:**
   - Verify unregistered commercial usage of "ROBUST-ADAPT" across PyPI, GitHub, and HuggingFace.

### 3.2 European Union (EUIPO) & United Kingdom (UKIPO)
1. **Relative Grounds for Refusal (EUTMR Art. 8 / UK TMA 1994 s. 5):**
   - Evaluate whether earlier marks containing "ADAPT" or "ROBUST" possess established reputation or high distinctive character.
2. **Direct Linguistic Meaning:**
   - Confirm mark does not consist exclusively of signs indicating quality or intended purpose (EUTMR Art. 7(1)(c)).

### 3.3 India (CGPDTM / Trade Marks Registry)
1. **Statutory Refusal Grounds under Trade Marks Act, 1999:**
   - **Section 9(1)(a):** Distinctiveness inquiry — ensure "ROBUST-ADAPT" is capable of distinguishing goods/services of one person from those of another.
   - **Section 11(1):** Relative grounds — evaluation of identical or similar marks on the Register of Trade Marks in Class 09 and 42 for similar goods.
   - **Section 11(2):** Check against well-known trademarks.
2. **Phonetic & Visual Search Parameters:**
   - Execute query `ROBUST` and `ADAPT` under Indian TMR Public Search with phonetic matching enabled.
3. **Common Law Passing Off (§ 27(2)):**
   - Assess whether "Adaptive ML Inc." has established protectable commercial goodwill in the Indian jurisdiction that could support an action for passing off.
